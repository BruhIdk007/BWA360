# Community References and Inspiration

BWA360/LexEngine uses community-maintained resources as **research indexes, discovery aids and creative inspiration**, while reconstruction-critical behavior is validated against the original games, executable/data analysis, traces and reproducible tests.

## Bookworm Adventures Wikia / Fandom

Community reference:

https://bookwormadvs.fandom.com/wiki/Bookworm_Adventures_Wikia

The project maintainer intends to consult this wiki especially for:

- creature/enemy and boss discovery;
- names, themes and encounter inspiration;
- characters, locations, items and terminology;
- high-level game progression and content cross-checking;
- finding details that are worth verifying directly in the original games.

It is also a useful community resource for people interested in Bookworm Adventures, so BWA360 should **link back to it rather than silently mirror it**. Readers of this repository are encouraged to visit the wiki and support the community knowledge base.

### Evidence rule

The wiki is not treated as executable-level ground truth. For claims that affect Classic reconstruction, compatibility, balance or engine behavior, verify against one or more of:

- the user's legally obtained original game files;
- disassembly/decompilation evidence;
- runtime traces and differential tests;
- original in-game behavior;
- other primary or independently corroborated sources.

### Content and attribution rule

Do not copy wiki text, screenshots, artwork or other hosted material into this repository unless its license and attribution requirements explicitly allow that use. Prefer a link plus a concise original summary. Proprietary PopCap/EA game assets remain outside the repository regardless of whether they also appear on a community wiki.

BWA360 is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Fandom or the maintainers of the Bookworm Adventures Wikia.
